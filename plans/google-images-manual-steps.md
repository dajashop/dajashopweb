# Google Images Manual Steps

## 1) Submit image sitemap in Search Console

1. Open Google Search Console for your domain.
2. Go to Sitemaps.
3. Submit sitemap URL (example: https://your-domain.com/sitemap.xml).
4. Wait for processing and check Indexed pages/images reports.

## 2) Use descriptive image file names

Use clear names before upload:

- Good: casio-gshock-ga2100-black.webp
- Bad: IMG_001.webp

## 3) Keep image size high enough

Recommended:

- Width at least 1200px for better Google Discover and image visibility.
- Keep quality balanced with compression (WebP/AVIF when possible).

## 4) Optional: Google Merchant Center

If products are sold online, connect Merchant Center and upload feeds.
Free listings can improve product image visibility in Google surfaces.

## 5) Alt text best practices

Use short, descriptive alt text:

- Include brand + product type
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Keep it natural and specific

Example:

- "Casio G-Shock muski rucni sat, crni model"
