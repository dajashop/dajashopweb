Why we use WhatsApp

News and offers, individual watch price alerts and automatic wishlist price tracking are separate, optional subscriptions. We use your consent to send them, and you can withdraw it at any time. For price alerts, you can confirm the request with a verified number on your account. For subscriptions that require WhatsApp confirmation, send the prepared message from your number. Signing in or making a purchase does not automatically subscribe you to WhatsApp marketing.

Data we process

We process your phone number, selected language, subscription and product, consent and opt-out records, event times, message identifiers and sending/delivery statuses. Our webhook processes reply text when it is used to confirm a subscription or unsubscribe. Sign-in uses a one-time code; our server stores only a protected verification value. We do not request your Facebook password through WhatsApp messages and do not sell this data.

Service providers

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to transmit messages and Render infrastructure for our server and database. Meta may process data outside Serbia under its applicable terms and privacy policy: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy. This notice covers the DajaShop WhatsApp flow and does not replace Meta's policy.

Retention and security

Sign-in codes expire after five minutes and subscription confirmation links after 30 minutes. Unconfirmed requests are removed after expiry and an additional day; received webhook event identifiers are removed after 30 days; queued messages expire after at most 23 hours. Active subscriptions are retained while you use them. Completed delivery records are removed after 90 days from their last status change. Revoked subscriptions and phone numbers are removed after 90 days from opt-out, once associated deliveries have finished and been removed. Limited evidence with a hashed phone number is kept for no more than 12 months from opt-out. This evidence is pseudonymised, not fully anonymous. Cleanup runs periodically. You may request earlier deletion, subject to applicable legal exceptions. Access is restricted and incoming Meta messages are verified with a digital signature.

Unsubscribing and deletion

In the DajaShop chat, send STOP for the newsletter, STOP PRICES for all price alerts and automatic wishlist tracking, or STOP ALL for all WhatsApp subscriptions. For one watch, reply to its message with STOP WATCH or send STOP WATCH 1042 with your own code. Send MY WATCHES for your codes or open your account settings. DajaShop charges no fee for opting out. The opt-out applies to future WhatsApp messages.

To opt out or exercise your rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, email info@dajashop.rs with the relevant number. We may ask you to verify that you control it. You may lodge a complaint with Serbia’s Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.
