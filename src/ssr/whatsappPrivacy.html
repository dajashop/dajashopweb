We process your phone number, selected language, subscription and product, consent and opt-out records, event times, message identifiers and sending/delivery statuses. We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to transmit messages and Render infrastructure for our server and database. Confirmed subscriptions, consent, opt-out and delivery records have no automatic deletion period in this flow; you may request their deletion. Access is restricted and incoming Meta messages are verified with a digital signature.
